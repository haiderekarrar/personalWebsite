What I've built

Voice AI Check-in Agent

Nobody wants to type a weekly update on their feet. But almost anyone can talk for one minute. This voice agent calls each person on a team, asks what they’re working on, how busy they are, and what’s holding them up. Then it logs their answers to the manager’s Google Sheet and suggests what the manager should do next.

I built it for deployment teams, the people who get a company’s software working for each new customer. But it works anywhere a manager needs updates from people out in the field, like service technicians, home health aides, and sales reps on the road.
